# PropTrack

> PropTrack is an Australian property data, analytics, and automated valuation business (part of REA Group). It supplies residential property data, an AI-powered Automated Valuation Model (AVM), the PropTrack Home Price Index, and market insights drawn from over a trillion property data points across ~12 million Australian residential properties. Its developer program exposes REST APIs — address suggestion/matching, property and listing data, sold-transaction search (point-and-radius), automated valuations, and market metrics — documented on a Stoplight developer portal and secured with OAuth 2.0.

## APIs

- [PropTrack APIs (developer portal)](https://developer.proptrack.com.au/docs/apis/home): REST APIs for Australian property intelligence — address, property/listing data, sold-transaction search, AVM valuations, and market data. OAuth 2.0.
